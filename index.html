03 The strategy

Four priorities.
One coherent programme.

A programme I will develop with the president and committee, connecting technical depth to practice, feedback, and work members can show.

01 / Advanced practice

Go deeper. Build further.

Peer- and faculty-led technical teardowns on difficult problems: low-resource NLP, efficient inference, data drift, and research-to-product pipelines.

02 / Competitive preparation

Build competition pods.

Small, opt-in teams pairing experienced competitors with first-time participants. Review validation, compare experiments, and dissect published solutions after challenges.

03 / Industry bridges

Bring the trade-offs into the room.

Invite practitioners from health, fintech, telecom, and logistics to unpack approved cases, production failures, and decisions about data, cost, and deployment.

04 / Creative community

Make room for different strengths.

Use visual storytelling, data forensics, and live sprints to connect analysts, researchers, engineers, and communicators through shared challenges.

The plan centres on 12 core gatherings. Competition pods can meet between events as members choose. I will seek partner support for a Data Sprint edition built around an approved industry problem.

Three ways to put the strategy into practice.

01 / VISUALISE

Data as art. Evidence as story.

DataCanvas

Turn African public data into one compelling visual story. Choose your tools, explain your methods, and exhibit work that helps people make sense of an important question.

Creativity + rigour7–10 days
02 / INVESTIGATE

Find the signal. Defend the answer.

Case X

A data forensics challenge with planted anomalies and hidden signals. Build statistical tests, trace the evidence, and explain what happened and what to do next.

Evidence + judgement2 hours
03 / EXPERIMENT

Build. Test. Defend.

Data Sprint

Four focused hours, a shared dataset, and a practical problem. Build a validated baseline, improve your pipeline, and finish with a two-minute pitch.

Speed + sound methods4 hours

  1. 01 Data drift & reliable evaluation

How will we make advanced sessions useful and accessible?

Preparation notes and starter materials help members arrive ready. Each lab centres on a specific problem and its trade-offs, with substantial hands-on work and a review of the evidence. Extension tasks support experienced members, while peer support and comparable compute limits keep participation practical. Practitioner cases can use public, synthetic, or approved company data.
